# Product headline and subheader copy

The frontend copy uses two layers:

- **Tagline:** a concise, outcome-focused statement with no terminal period
- **Description:** a more detailed explanation of the workflow, evidence model, and deliverable

## Legacy Parity Room

**Tagline:** Recover hidden business rules and prove that modernized systems preserve required behavior

**Description:** Trace rules to code, SQL, documents, runtime evidence, and expert review, then turn approved findings into regression tests and parity packets

## Agent-Safe Data Gateway

**Tagline:** Give AI agents useful, governed access without exposing the underlying database

**Description:** Define purpose-bound data contracts, minimize sensitive fields, enforce query and cost limits, and review every attempted action in a complete ledger

## Public-Data Evidence Room

**Tagline:** Verify that every material claim is supported by current, correctly scoped evidence

**Description:** Connect report language to source versions, geographies, periods, transformations, and reviewer decisions while flagging stale, contradictory, or overstated conclusions

## Knowledge Succession Room

**Tagline:** Capture critical operational knowledge before experienced staff retire, transfer, or leave

**Description:** Turn interviews, documents, demonstrations, and workflow evidence into validated transition packets, unresolved-gap registers, and successor-readiness exercises

## Policy Impact Mapper

**Tagline:** Translate a policy change into affected systems, forms, workflows, and owners

**Description:** Extract source-linked requirements, map operational and technology impacts, surface ambiguities and dependencies, assign accountable owners, and build an implementation packet
